Document Overview
BASNY ENTERPRISE requires a mobile first online store for footwear, bags and related fashion products. Customers must be 
able to discover products, choose the correct colour and size, add items to a cart, pay securely, receive order updates and submit 
verified reviews. Administrators must be able to manage the store without routine developer support.
Core Outcome
The finished deliverable must be a fully manageable retail platform, not only a product display website. BASNY ENTERPRISE 
must be able to control products, variants, prices, stock, promotions, customers, orders, payments, delivery, reviews, website 
content and reports from the administration dashboard.

1 Purpose and System Requirements
1.1 Business Scope
The platform will support the sale of women's fashion products, including heels, flat shoes, sandals, slippers, sneakers, handbags, 
shoulder bags, crossbody bags, tote bags, clutches and other accessories. The design and data model must allow new categories 
and product types to be introduced later without rebuilding the platform.
1.2 General Requirements
 Responsive operation on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.
 Mobile first interface optimized for customers arriving from Instagram, WhatsApp and other social channels.
 Clean, modern fashion retail presentation with fast product discovery and clear calls to action.
 Fast page loading, optimized product images and reliable performance on typical mobile data connections.
 Secure HTTPS deployment, validated input and protected customer and administrator accounts.
 Simple administration suitable for non technical staff.
 Search engine friendly page structure and product URLs.
 Modular architecture that allows future payment, delivery and notification integrations.
1.3 User Roles

3 Products Variants and Inventory
3.1 Product Record
Data group Required fields
Identification Product name, SKU or product code, category, subcategory, status
Description Short description, full description, material and brand where applicable
Pricing Selling price, original price, promotional price and discount status
Media Main image, multiple gallery images, image order and optional colour specific images
Variants Colour, size and any future selectable attribute
Inventory Quantity by variation, low stock threshold and availability
Merchandising Featured, new arrival, best seller, sale and visibility status
3.2 Variation Level Inventory
Stock must be maintained for each sellable colour and size combination. A single total stock value for a footwear style is not 
sufficient. Customers must not be allowed to purchase an unavailable combination.
Product variation Available stock
Black heel Size 37 3
Black heel Size 38 5
Black heel Size 39 0
Nude heel Size 38 2
3.3 Size and Colour Management
 Footwear sizes must be reusable structured options, including sizes 35 to 42 and other sizes added later.
 Each size may have a separate stock quantity for every colour.
 Each colour may have its own product image, sizes, SKU and stock quantities.
 The storefront must update images, availability and price when a variation is selected where applicable.
3.4 Inventory Controls
 Add, reduce and correct stock with an adjustment reason.
 View stock by product, colour and size.
 Automatically reduce stock after confirmed purchase according to the agreed reservation rule.
 Restore stock when an order is cancelled or refunded, subject to administrator confirmation.
 Display low stock and out of stock items and optionally email low stock alerts.
 Prevent overselling through server side stock validation during checkout and payment confirmation.
Recommended rule: reserve stock for a limited period when checkout begins and release it automatically if payment is not 
confirmed. The exact reservation period must be agreed during implementation.
3.5 Product Image Management
 Multiple high quality photos per product.
 Automatic image compression and thumbnail generation.
 Image reordering and main image selection.
 Mobile optimized image delivery and lazy loading.
 File type and size validation; administrators should not need to manually resize every image.

4 Cart Checkout and Payments
4.1 Shopping Cart
 Add products with the selected size, colour and quantity.
 View, update and remove cart items.
 Change a variation, subject to availability.
 Apply coupon codes and display the resulting discount.
 Display subtotal, estimated delivery fee, discount and order total.
 Retain cart contents when the customer leaves temporarily, within the defined retention period.
4.2 Checkout
Information group Fields and behaviour
Customer Full name, phone number and email address
Delivery Region, city or town, area, address, landmark and delivery instructions
Order summary Products, images, variants, quantity, subtotal, discount, delivery fee and final total
Consent Acceptance of applicable terms, delivery and return conditions before order submission
Guest checkout should be available. A customer who chooses to register should be able to save addresses and review order 
history.
4.3 Online Payment
 Integrate one agreed Ghana compatible gateway such as Paystack or another approved provider.
 Support Mobile Money, Visa, Mastercard and other methods available through the selected gateway.
 Create unique transaction references and associate each transaction with one order.
 Record method, amount, status, date, time and gateway reference.
 Verify successful payments on the server using the provider's verification mechanism or webhook.
 Never mark an order paid solely because the customer's browser reached a success page.
 Do not store raw payment card details within the BASNY ENTERPRISE system.
Payment status Meaning
Pending Payment started but not confirmed
Successful Verified by the gateway
Failed Payment attempt did not complete
Refunded Full refund recorded
Partially Refunded Part of the paid amount returned where supported
4.4 Manual Payment Options
The administrator may enable or disable bank transfer, manual Mobile Money transfer or pay on pickup. Orders using a manual 
method must remain in Awaiting Payment Verification until an authorized administrator approves or rejects the payment. Any 
uploaded proof must be validated as a secure file upload.

WhatsApp support must remain available throughout the storefront. From a product page, the prefilled message should include 
the product name and link, and may include the chosen size or colour. Example: Hello BASNY ENTERPRISE, I would like 
more information about the Black Heel in Size 39.
6.5 Abandoned Carts
Where practical, the system should record carts or checkout sessions that were not completed. The feature must respect 
applicable privacy requirements and should support later remarketing without automatically enrolling customers in promotional
messaging.

10 Security Architecture and Quality
10.1 Minimum Security Controls
 HTTPS and valid SSL certificate across the entire website.
 Strong password hashing and secure reset tokens.
 Protection against SQL injection, cross site scripting and cross site request forgery where applicable.
 Server side authorization for every administrator action.
 Login rate limiting and secure session management.
 Input validation and output encoding.
 Secure image and proof of payment upload validation.
 Payment gateway signature or webhook verification.
 Automated and tested database backups.
 Secrets stored outside public source code and production logs.
 Audit logging for sensitive administration actions.
10.2 Data and Payment Protection
The platform must collect only information necessary for ordering and support. Raw card numbers, CVV values and full payment 
credentials must never be stored. Payment processing must remain within the selected gateway's secure environment. Customer 
exports and reports must be restricted to authorized roles.
10.3 Performance and Reliability
 Optimize images and static assets for mobile delivery.
 Use pagination or efficient loading for long catalogues.
 Provide clear error states without exposing internal technical information.
 Use database constraints and transactions to protect order, payment and stock consistency.
 Maintain backups and document the restoration process.

12 User Workflows and Acceptance Criteria
12.1 Customer Purchase Workflow
10. Customer opens the homepage and browses or searches for a product.
11. Customer opens the product page and selects an available colour and size.
12. Customer adds the variation to the cart or uses Buy Now.
13. Customer reviews the cart, applies any valid coupon and proceeds to checkout.
14. Customer enters contact and delivery information and chooses delivery or pickup.
15. Customer selects a payment method and completes payment.
16. The server verifies payment and creates or updates the order correctly.
17. Customer receives confirmation and can follow the order status.
18. BASNY ENTERPRISE processes, dispatches or prepares the order for pickup.
19. After completion, an eligible customer may submit a product review.
12.2 Minimum Acceptance Tests
No Test Pass condition
1 Admin creates a product with category, price, size and colour. Product appears correctly on the storefront.
2 Admin enters stock for individual colour and size combinations. Each variation shows the correct availability.
3 Customer searches, filters and opens a product. Relevant products and details are returned.
4 Customer selects an available variation and adds it to cart. Cart retains the exact product, colour, size, quantity and 
price.
5 Customer attempts an unavailable variation. Purchase is blocked with a clear message.
6 Customer completes checkout and successful payment. Verified payment produces a paid order with a unique 
number.
7 Payment fails or is abandoned. No falsely paid order is created and stock is released 
according to the agreed rule.
8 A confirmed order is processed. Stock reduces once and the admin can view full order 
details.
9 Admin changes the order status. Customer view and notification reflect the change.
10 Customer submits a review after purchase. Review enters moderation and can be approved with 
Verified Purchase status.
11 Admin creates a coupon and scheduled promotion. Rules, dates and usage limits are enforced correctly.
12 Admin updates a homepage banner and policy page. Changes publish without developer intervention.
13 Unauthorized user attempts a protected action. Server denies access and records the event where 
appropriate.
14 Website is tested on mobile, tablet and desktop. No clipped content, broken controls or unusable checkout 
steps.
15 Order and payment reports are exported. Exported records match filtered dashboard data.
16 Backup and recovery procedure is demonstrated or documented. Critical store data can be restored through the agreed 
process.
